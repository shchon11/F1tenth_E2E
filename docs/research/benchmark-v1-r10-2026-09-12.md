# Checkpoint benchmark v1 — R10 directional-coverage checkpoints

**2026-09-12.** The two R10 runs (`cl_r10_base_s701`, `cl_r10_base_s702`) scored against the same
frozen suite v1 as the [published three-system table](benchmark-v1-2026-09-12.md). Same 34 cells,
272 trials per system, static friction per episode, `estimated` controller arm with estimator seed 401.

R10 differs from the SGR `base` recipe in exactly three flags: `--name`, `--wandb-group`, and
`--tracks`, which adds the reversed direction of each of the five training maps (10 routes, same five
terrain grids, float32 preserved). Both runs started from the frozen original weights, 128 updates /
1 048 576 steps each. W&B: [`s1fctkkq`](https://wandb.ai/shchon11-hanyang-university/f1sim-e2e/runs/s1fctkkq),
[`qrww37cd`](https://wandb.ai/shchon11-hanyang-university/f1sim-e2e/runs/qrww37cd).

## Headline

**R10 does not improve on the frozen original under the estimated controller, and regresses on
low-friction solo driving.** Seed 701 matches the frozen original on avoidance and is within one
race on overtaking; seed 702 is below it on both. Neither seed beats it anywhere. Solo lap time on
commonly completed trials is about 0.2 s faster, the same pattern the earlier `cl_main_estimated_s501`
retrain showed. Training on reversed directions did not recover what the five-map solo recipe loses.

measured under different digests. The only difference is the `n_envs` producer fix, which changes
what the row file records, not what is measured; the published rows carry the same key via the
documented derived extract. The rows are therefore comparable, but the reporter is not asked to say
so: the R10 table below is the reporter's verbatim output for the two new systems, and the
cross-system comparison after it is computed directly from each row's `tally`, which reproduces the
published reporter columns exactly for the three earlier systems (12.069 / 6.067 / 6.955 collisions
per km, 110 / 134 / 135 completions).

## Reading

- The reversed-direction data did not change the picture set by the SGR screen and the s501 retrain:
  every retrain on the five-map solo recipe ends at or below `frozen_original@estimated` on this
  suite, with a small pace gain paid for in low-µ completion and slip.
- Seed dependence is again visible: 701 and 702 differ by 5 of 64 on avoidance and 3 of 64 on
  overtaking with identical recipes.
- Scope as before: three reused development maps, no unseen-map or on-car claim. The R10 training
  maps (ICRA, BlackBox, Spielberg) are not in this suite, so a training-map gain would not show here;
  the SGR screen measured that separately for the non-reversed recipe.
